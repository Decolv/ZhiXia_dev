# 高级难度长难句

## 句子1
### 原句
It is not the consciousness of men that determines their being, but, on the contrary, their social being that determines their consciousness—a formulation that, while acknowledging the dialectical interplay between material conditions and ideological superstructures, privileges the former as the ultimately decisive factor in historical development.

### 翻译
不是人们的意识决定他们的存在，相反，是他们的社会存在决定他们的意识——这一表述虽然在承认物质条件与意识形态上层建筑之间的辩证相互作用的同时，但将前者视为历史发展中最终的决定性因素。

### 语法分析
这是一个复杂的强调句"It is...that..."，强调部分使用了"not...but..."的并列结构。破折号后的"a formulation..."是同位语，对前面的哲学命题进行补充说明。其中"that..."是定语从句修饰"formulation"，从句中嵌套了"while acknowledging..."这一现在分词短语作让步状语，以及"as the ultimately decisive factor..."这一介词短语作宾语补足语。

### 重点词汇
- consciousness: 意识
- dialectical: 辩证的
- interplay: 相互作用
- material conditions: 物质条件
- ideological superstructures: 意识形态上层建筑
- privilege: 给予特权，优先考虑

## 句子2
### 原句
Scarcely had the implications of the new regulatory framework begun to be absorbed by market participants when fresh guidance from the oversight body, issued in response to lobbying pressure from industry groups, introduced additional compliance requirements that threatened to render obsolete the business models upon which many firms had predicated their growth strategies.

### 翻译
市场参与者刚开始领会新监管框架的影响，监管机构就针对行业团体的游说压力发布了新的指导意见，引入了额外的合规要求，这可能使许多公司赖以制定增长战略的商业模式变得过时。

### 语法分析
这是一个复杂的倒装句，"Scarcely...when..."结构置于句首引起部分倒装"had...begun"。主句中"issued in response to..."是过去分词短语作后置定语修饰"guidance"。"that threatened to render obsolete..."是定语从句修饰"requirements"，其中"upon which..."是定语从句修饰"business models"，使用了"predicated upon"（以……为基础）这一搭配。

### 重点词汇
- regulatory framework: 监管框架
- oversight body: 监管机构
- lobbying pressure: 游说压力
- compliance requirements: 合规要求
- render obsolete: 使过时
- predicate: 使基于

## 句子3
### 原句
What renders this particular instance of institutional failure especially troubling is not merely the magnitude of the financial losses incurred, grievous though they were, but rather the revelation that those entrusted with safeguarding the public interest had, through a combination of negligence and willful blindness, allowed a situation to develop that would have been readily preventable had elementary precautions been observed.

### 翻译
使这一特定制度失效案例特别令人不安的，不仅仅是所遭受的财务损失的巨大程度（尽管损失确实严重），而是这样一个事实的揭露：那些被委以维护公共利益重任的人，由于疏忽和故意视而不见，任由一种本可以通过采取基本预防措施而轻易避免的情况发展起来。

### 语法分析
主语从句"What renders...troubling"作主语。表语部分使用了"not merely...but rather..."的递进结构。"grievous though they were"是倒装的让步状语从句，相当于"though they were grievous"。"that those entrusted..."是同位语从句，其中"entrusted with..."是过去分词短语作定语，"had allowed"是过去完成时，"that would have been preventable..."是定语从句修饰"situation"，其中又嵌套了虚拟语气倒装"had...been observed"。

### 重点词汇
- institutional failure: 制度失效
- grievous: 严重的，令人悲痛的
- willful blindness: 故意视而不见
- elementary precautions: 基本预防措施
- entrust with: 委托，委以
- negligence: 疏忽

## 句子4
### 原句
Only by recognizing that the apparent stability of the pre-crisis period was, in fact, predicated upon an unsustainable accumulation of leverage—one that masked underlying vulnerabilities while creating perverse incentives for risk-taking—can we hope to construct a financial architecture sufficiently resilient to withstand the inevitable shocks that cyclical economic processes periodically generate.

### 翻译
只有认识到危机前时期的表面稳定实际上建立在不可持续的杠杆积累之上——这种积累掩盖了潜在的脆弱性，同时为冒险行为创造了反常的激励——我们才有希望构建一个足够有弹性的金融架构，以承受周期性经济过程定期产生的不可避免的冲击。

### 语法分析
这是一个复杂的倒装句，"Only by recognizing..."置于句首引起部分倒装"can we hope"。"that the apparent stability..."是宾语从句作recognizing的宾语，其中"predicated upon..."是形容词短语。破折号内是"one that..."这一同位语结构，"that masked...while creating..."是定语从句修饰"one"。主句中"sufficiently resilient to withstand..."是形容词短语作后置定语修饰"architecture"，其中"that cyclical...generate"是定语从句修饰"shocks"。

### 重点词汇
- unsustainable: 不可持续的
- leverage: 杠杆
- mask: 掩盖
- perverse incentives: 反常激励
- resilient: 有弹性的，能复原的
- cyclical: 周期性的

## 句子5
### 原句
Were it not for the fact that the empirical evidence, however compelling in isolation, fails to account for the confounding variables that alternative theoretical frameworks suggest may be operative—variables that, if incorporated into the analytical model, would substantially alter the inferred causal relationships—one might be tempted to accept the conclusions drawn by the study's authors at face value.

### 翻译
如果不是因为这样一个事实：尽管单独来看实证证据似乎很有说服力，但它未能考虑到替代理论框架所暗示的可能在起作用的混杂变量——如果将这些变量纳入分析模型，将会实质性地改变所推断的因果关系——人们可能会倾向于不加质疑地接受研究作者得出的结论。

### 语法分析
这是一个极其复杂的虚拟语气倒装句，"Were it not for..."相当于"If it were not for..."，表示与现在事实相反的假设。"that the empirical evidence..."是同位语从句，其中"however compelling in isolation"是插入的让步状语。"that alternative...suggest may be operative"是定语从句修饰"variables"，其中"may be operative"是省略了that的宾语从句。破折号内是"variables that..."这一同位语结构，其中"if incorporated..."是省略的虚拟条件句，"would substantially alter..."是主句。

### 重点词汇
- empirical evidence: 实证证据
- compelling: 有说服力的
- confounding variables: 混杂变量
- theoretical frameworks: 理论框架
- operative: 起作用的
- at face value: 表面价值，不加质疑地
