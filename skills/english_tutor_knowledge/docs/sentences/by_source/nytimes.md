# 纽约时报外刊长难句

## 句子1
### 原句
What began as a localized protest against a proposed extradition law has evolved into a broader movement demanding democratic reforms, posing the most significant challenge to Beijing's authority in the territory since the handover in 1997.

### 翻译
最初只是针对一项引渡法案的地方性抗议，已经演变成一场要求民主改革的更广泛运动，对北京在该地区的权威构成了自1997年回归以来最重大的挑战。

### 语法分析
主语从句"What began as..."作主语，谓语为"has evolved into"，"posing..."是现在分词短语作结果状语。句末"demanding..."是现在分词短语作后置定语修饰"movement"，"since..."是时间状语。

### 重点词汇
- localized: 地方性的，局部的
- extradition: 引渡
- evolve into: 演变成
- handover: 移交，回归
- territory: 领土，地区

## 句子2
### 原句
So pervasive has social media become in shaping public discourse that political campaigns now allocate a significant portion of their budgets to digital advertising, often targeting micro-communities with messages tailored to their specific concerns and values.

### 翻译
社交媒体在塑造公共话语方面已经变得如此普遍，以至于政治竞选活动现在将预算的很大一部分分配给数字广告，经常针对微型社区发送符合其特定关切和价值观的信息。

### 语法分析
这是一个"so...that..."结构的倒装句，"So pervasive"置于句首引起部分倒装"has social media become"。"that"引导结果状语从句，其中"targeting..."是现在分词短语作伴随状语，"tailored to..."是过去分词短语作后置定语修饰"messages"。

### 重点词汇
- pervasive: 普遍的，无处不在的
- discourse:  discourse，谈话
- allocate: 分配，配置
- micro-communities: 微型社区
- tailor: 定制，使适应

## 句子3
### 原句
The legislation, which had been stalled in committee for nearly two years amid partisan bickering, finally passed after lawmakers reached a compromise that satisfied neither side completely but was deemed preferable to the prospect of no action at all.

### 翻译
这项立法在党派争吵中在委员会搁置了近两年，最终在立法者达成妥协后获得通过——这个妥协虽然没有完全满足任何一方，但被认为比完全不采取行动更可取。

### 语法分析
主句为"The legislation...finally passed"，"which had been stalled..."是非限制性定语从句修饰"legislation"。"after"引导时间状语从句，其中"that satisfied..."是定语从句修饰"compromise"，"but was deemed..."与"satisfied"并列，"preferable to..."是形容词短语。

### 重点词汇
- stall: 搁置，停滞
- partisan: 党派的，偏袒的
- bickering: 争吵，口角
- compromise: 妥协
- deem: 认为，视为

## 句子4
### 原句
Rarely has the gap between scientific consensus and public understanding been so stark as in the debate over climate change, where well-established findings about the role of human activity are routinely dismissed by those who view environmental regulation as an assault on economic freedom.

### 翻译
科学共识与公众理解之间的差距很少像在气候变化辩论中那样明显，在那里，关于人类活动作用的已确立发现经常被那些将环境法规视为对经济自由攻击的人所否定。

### 语法分析
倒装句，"Rarely"置于句首引起部分倒装"has...been"。"as in the debate..."是比较状语。"where"引导非限制性定语从句修饰"debate"，其中"who view..."是定语从句修饰"those"，"as an assault..."是介词短语作宾语补足语。

### 重点词汇
- consensus: 共识，一致意见
- stark: 明显的，鲜明的
- well-established: 已确立的，公认的
- dismiss: 否定，驳回
- assault: 攻击，抨击

## 句子5
### 原句
It was only after investigators subpoenaed thousands of pages of internal documents—emails, memos, and financial records—that the full scope of the company's efforts to conceal its knowledge of the product's dangers became clear to the public.

### 翻译
只有在调查人员传唤了数千页内部文件——电子邮件、备忘录和财务记录——之后，该公司隐瞒其对产品危险知情程度的全部范围才为公众所知。

### 语法分析
强调句"It was...that..."，强调的是时间状语"only after investigators subpoenaed..."，其中包含同位语"emails, memos, and financial records"对"documents"进行补充说明。主句为"the full scope...became clear"，"of the company's efforts..."是介词短语作定语，"to conceal..."是不定式作定语修饰"efforts"。

### 重点词汇
- subpoena: 传唤， subpoena
- internal documents: 内部文件
- memo: 备忘录
- conceal: 隐瞒，隐藏
- scope: 范围，程度
