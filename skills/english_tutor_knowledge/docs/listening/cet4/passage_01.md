# 图书馆借书

## 原文
W: Excuse me, I'd like to borrow this book. Can you help me?

M: Sure. Do you have a library card?

W: Yes, here it is.

M: Let me check... Oh, I'm sorry. This book has been reserved by another student. You can borrow it next week.

W: That's too bad. How long can I keep the books I borrow?

M: Usually two weeks. But you can renew them online if you need more time.

W: Great. And what are the library hours?

M: We open at 8 a.m. and close at 10 p.m. on weekdays. On weekends, we close at 6 p.m.

W: I see. Thank you for your help!

M: You're welcome. Have a nice day!

## 翻译
女：打扰一下，我想借这本书。你能帮我吗？

男：当然。你有借书证吗？

女：有的，给你。

男：让我查一下……哦，抱歉。这本书已经被另一位学生预约了。你下周可以来借。

女：太遗憾了。我借的书可以借多久？

男：通常是两周。但如果你需要更多时间，可以在网上续借。

女：太好了。图书馆的开放时间是什么？

男：我们工作日上午8点开门，晚上10点关门。周末我们晚上6点关门。

女：我明白了。谢谢你的帮助！

男：不客气。祝你今天愉快！
