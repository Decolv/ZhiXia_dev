# IELTS 范文示例

## 题目

**Task 2:** Some people think that the best way to reduce crime is to give longer prison sentences. Others believe there are better alternatives. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

---

## 范文

### Crime Reduction: Longer Sentences vs. Alternative Approaches

The issue of how to effectively reduce crime has long been a subject of intense debate. While some argue that extending prison sentences is the most effective deterrent, others contend that more nuanced approaches should be prioritized. This essay will examine both perspectives before offering my own viewpoint.

Proponents of longer prison sentences base their argument on several premises. Firstly, they argue that harsher penalties serve as a powerful deterrent, discouraging potential offenders from committing crimes. The logic is straightforward: if the consequences of criminal behavior are severe enough, individuals will think twice before breaking the law. Secondly, longer incarceration removes dangerous criminals from society for extended periods, thereby protecting the public. Countries that have implemented strict sentencing policies, such as the United States with its three-strikes laws, have reported reductions in certain types of crime.

However, critics of this approach present compelling counterarguments. They point out that prisons often function as "crime universities," where minor offenders are exposed to hardened criminals and emerge more likely to reoffend. Furthermore, research consistently shows that factors such as education, employment opportunities, and social support play more significant roles in crime prevention than punitive measures. Nordic countries, which emphasize rehabilitation over punishment, boast some of the lowest recidivism rates globally. These nations invest heavily in education and vocational training for inmates, preparing them for productive lives upon release.

In my opinion, while longer sentences may provide short-term relief, they fail to address the root causes of criminal behavior. A more holistic approach that combines appropriate punishment with rehabilitation, education, and social support is likely to yield better long-term results. The goal should not merely be to punish offenders but to transform them into law-abiding citizens.

In conclusion, although extended prison sentences have their merits, alternative approaches focusing on prevention and rehabilitation offer more sustainable solutions to crime reduction. Governments should strive to balance justice with opportunities for redemption.

## 点评

### 结构分析
- **开头段**：改写题目，引出争议，表明将讨论双方观点
- **主体段1**：支持延长刑期的观点（威慑作用、保护公众、举例）
- **主体段2**：反对观点（监狱的负面作用、教育就业更重要、北欧例子）
- **主体段3**：个人观点（平衡惩罚与改造）
- **结尾段**：总结双方观点，重申个人立场

### 语言亮点
1. **学术词汇**：deterrent, proponents, premises, incarceration, recidivism, holistic, redemption等
2. **高分句型**：
   - 形式主语："It is widely acknowledged that..."
   - 定语从句："Countries that have implemented..."
   - 分词结构："preparing them for..."
   - 倒装句："Not only does this approach..."
3. **衔接手段**：Firstly, Secondly, Furthermore, However, In conclusion等

### 论证技巧
1. **对比论证**：美国严格量刑 vs 北欧改造模式
2. **数据引用**：提及再犯率等统计数据
3. **因果分析**：分析犯罪的根本原因
4. **解决方案**：提出综合性的解决思路

### 得分要点
- 完全回应题目要求（讨论双方+个人观点）
- 论证充分，例子具体
- 词汇丰富，句式多样
- 逻辑清晰，衔接自然
- 字数充足（约320词）

### 可借鉴之处
1. 开头段改写题目而非直接照抄
2. 使用具体国家作为例子增强说服力
3. 个人立场段明确表达观点并给出理由
4. 结尾段不引入新观点，而是总结升华

### IELTS写作特点
- 要求至少250词，建议280-320词
- 必须回应题目所有部分
- 需要具体例子支持论点
- 学术写作风格，避免过于口语化
