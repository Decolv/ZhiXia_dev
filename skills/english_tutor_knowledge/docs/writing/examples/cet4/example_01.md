# CET-4 范文示例

## 题目

**Directions:** For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write a short essay on the importance of reading ability and how to develop it. You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.

---

## 范文

### The Importance of Reading Ability and How to Develop It

In the digital age, reading ability has become more crucial than ever before. It not only serves as a fundamental skill for academic success but also opens the door to knowledge and personal growth.

First and foremost, strong reading ability enables us to access information efficiently. In an era of information explosion, the ability to read quickly and comprehend deeply gives us a competitive edge. Moreover, reading broadens our horizons by exposing us to different cultures, ideas, and perspectives. It enriches our minds and enhances our critical thinking skills.

To develop reading ability, several strategies can be employed. Firstly, we should cultivate a regular reading habit by setting aside dedicated time each day. Consistency is key to improvement. Secondly, we ought to read widely across various genres, from newspapers and magazines to novels and academic papers. This diversity helps us adapt to different writing styles. Thirdly, active reading techniques, such as taking notes and summarizing main ideas, can significantly boost comprehension.

In conclusion, reading ability is an invaluable asset in modern society. By practicing consistently and employing effective strategies, we can continuously enhance this essential skill and reap its lifelong benefits.

---

## 点评

### 结构分析
- **开头段**：引入话题，说明阅读能力的重要性
- **主体段1**：从信息获取和视野拓展两个角度论证重要性
- **主体段2**：提出三点具体建议（养成习惯、广泛阅读、主动阅读）
- **结尾段**：总结全文，强调持续练习的重要性

### 语言亮点
1. **词汇丰富**：crucial, fundamental, competitive edge, horizons, cultivate, invaluable等高级词汇
2. **句式多样**：使用倒装句（not only... but also）、定语从句、分词结构等
3. **连接词使用**：First and foremost, Moreover, Firstly, Secondly, Thirdly, In conclusion等使文章衔接流畅

### 得分要点
- 内容切题，论点清晰
- 结构完整，层次分明
- 语言准确，表达流畅
- 字数控制在要求范围内（约170词）

### 可借鉴之处
1. 使用"In the digital age"等时代背景引入话题
2. 主体段落采用"论点+论据"的结构
3. 建议部分使用序数词使条理清晰
4. 结尾段呼应开头，形成闭环
