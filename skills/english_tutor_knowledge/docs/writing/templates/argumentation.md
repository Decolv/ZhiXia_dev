# 议论文模板 (Argumentation Template)

## 结构框架

### 第一段：引言 (Introduction)
- **背景引入**：描述与话题相关的社会现象或背景
- **争议焦点**：提出争议性问题
- **中心论点**：明确表达自己的观点

### 第二段：主体段落1 (Body Paragraph 1)
- **主题句**：第一个分论点
- **论证**：举例、数据、引用等支持材料
- **分析**：解释论证与论点的关系

### 第三段：主体段落2 (Body Paragraph 2)
- **主题句**：第二个分论点
- **论证**：支持材料
- **分析**：深入分析

### 第四段：让步/反驳 (Concession/Refutation)
- **承认对立观点**：承认反方观点有一定道理
- **反驳**：说明为什么自己的观点更有说服力

### 第五段：结论 (Conclusion)
- **总结**：重申主要论点
- **升华**：提出建议或展望未来

## 常用表达

### 开头句
- In recent years, the issue of... has sparked a heated debate.
- With the rapid development of..., more and more people are concerned about...
- It is universally acknowledged that...
- Nowadays, there is a growing awareness that...

### 表达观点
- From my perspective, I firmly believe that...
- As far as I am concerned, I am in favor of...
- I am convinced that...
- It seems to me that...

### 论证连接
- First and foremost, ...
- Furthermore, ...
- Moreover, ...
- In addition, ...
- What is more, ...

### 举例说明
- For instance, ...
- A case in point is...
- Take... for example.
- This can be illustrated by...

### 让步转折
- Admittedly, ... However, ...
- It is true that..., but...
- While it may be true that..., I believe...
- Despite the fact that..., ...

### 结尾句
- In conclusion, ...
- To sum up, ...
- Taking all these factors into consideration, ...
- Only in this way can we...
