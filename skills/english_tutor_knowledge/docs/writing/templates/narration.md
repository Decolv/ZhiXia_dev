# 记叙文模板 (Narration Template)

## 结构框架

### 第一段：开头 (Beginning)
- **场景设定**：时间、地点、人物
- **背景介绍**：交代故事发生的背景
- **引入事件**：引出要叙述的事件

### 第二段：发展 (Development)
- **事件经过**：按时间顺序叙述事件的发展
- **细节描写**：动作、语言、心理、环境描写
- **情节推进**：冲突或转折的出现

### 第三段：高潮 (Climax)
- **关键情节**：故事最精彩或最重要的部分
- **情感爆发**：人物情感的最高点
- **问题解决**：矛盾或困难的解决过程

### 第四段：结局 (Ending)
- **结果交代**：事件的最终结果
- **感悟升华**：从事件中得到的启示或感悟
- **呼应开头**：与开头形成呼应

## 常用表达

### 时间顺序
- It was a sunny/cold/rainy day when...
- One day, ...
- Last summer/winter, ...
- Years ago, ...
- At that moment, ...

### 场景描写
- The sun was shining brightly.
- The wind was blowing gently.
- It was getting dark.
- The room was filled with...

### 动作描写
- Suddenly, ...
- Without hesitation, ...
- Rushing to..., I...
- Holding my breath, I...

### 心理描写
- I felt a mixture of... and...
- My heart was pounding with...
- A sense of... washed over me.
- I couldn't help but feel...

### 对话引入
- "...," he said with a smile.
- "...," she whispered.
- "...," I replied confidently.

### 情感表达
- To my surprise/joy/disappointment, ...
- I was overwhelmed with emotion.
- Tears welled up in my eyes.
- A wave of relief flooded over me.

### 结尾升华
- From this experience, I learned that...
- It was then that I realized...
- This unforgettable experience taught me...
- Looking back, I understand that...

## 高分技巧

### 1. 感官描写
- **视觉**：colors, shapes, sizes
- **听觉**：sounds, noises, voices
- **嗅觉**：smells, scents, odors
- **触觉**：textures, temperatures
- **味觉**：flavors, tastes

### 2. 修辞手法
- **比喻**：as... as..., like...
- **拟人**：赋予无生命物体以人的特征
- **排比**：parallel structure
- **夸张**：hyperbole

### 3. 句式变化
- 长短句结合
- 简单句与复合句交替
- 使用分词短语作状语
- 适当使用倒装句

### 4. 过渡词
- 时间：meanwhile, shortly after, soon, later
- 因果：therefore, as a result, consequently
- 转折：however, nevertheless, yet
- 递进：besides, in addition, furthermore
